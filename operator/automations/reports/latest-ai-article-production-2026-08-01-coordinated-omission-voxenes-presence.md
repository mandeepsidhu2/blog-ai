## Deep Evidence

Created `coordinated-omission-inference-audit` with a version-1 evidence
manifest, fixed configuration, dependency-free runner, README, research log,
320 repeat-level rows per scenario, aggregate results, 5,000-sample paired
bootstrap intervals, claim table, SHA-256 ledger, generated SVG, and the
preserved failed exploratory outputs.

The confirmatory design compares evenly paced open-loop arrivals with 16
closed-loop workers for 60 seconds after a 10-second warmup. It uses a single
FCFS lane, lognormal 9 ms base service, a 100 ms p99 SLO, 320 matched repeats,
and four cells: no pause at 80 requests/s, a 400 ms pause every 20 seconds at
80 requests/s, an 800 ms pause every 20 seconds at 80 requests/s, and the same
800 ms pause at 100 requests/s.

In the focal 80 requests/s bursty-pause cell, open-loop median p99 was 751.55
ms and failed every repeat. Naive closed-loop median p99 was 41.33 ms and
passed 57.5% of repeats while achieving 76.56 requests/s. Interval-corrected
closed-loop median p99 was 772.18 ms. The paired naive closed-minus-open p99
delta was -404.40 ms [95% bootstrap interval -442.69, -366.96]; the corrected
delta was +20.55 ms [18.90, 22.19]. The no-pause control passed under both
generators, although its nonzero p99 difference demonstrates that the harnesses
are not interchangeable.

The preregistered prediction that at least 75% of focal repeats would falsely
pass was falsified at 57.5%. The article preserves that negative result and
limits its conclusion to the measured decision reversal. The initial Poisson
open-loop screen was also rejected and preserved because arrival burstiness
confounded the no-pause control.
